🔒 Privacy Policy - 8Eggs

Last Updated: December 27, 2024

📱 About 8Eggs

8Eggs is a mobile game where players catch falling eggs. This policy applies to both our mobile app and invitation website.

📊 Data We Collect

In the 8Eggs App:

  • Player Display Name: Chosen by you for leaderboards
  • Anonymous User ID: Generated for game progress tracking
  • Game Progress: Scores, levels, achievements
  • Device Information: Basic device info for compatibility
  • Purchase Records: In-app purchase receipts (processed by Google Play)

On Our Website (invite.888solutions.info):

  • Friend Referral Codes: From URL parameters for invitation tracking
  • Install Click Events: To track successful installations
  • Browser Information: For website compatibility

💾 How We Store Data

All data is stored securely in Supabase databases with:

  • 🔐 Encryption in transit: HTTPS/SSL for all data transfers
  • 🔒 Encryption at rest: Database-level encryption
  • 📱 Local Storage: Some game data stored locally on your device

🎯 How We Use Data

Data is used solely for:

  • ✅ Game functionality and progress tracking
  • ✅ Leaderboard rankings and competition
  • ✅ Friend referral system rewards
  • ✅ App improvement and bug fixes
  • ✅ Processing in-app purchases (via Google Play)

🚫 What We Don't Do

  • ❌ We do NOT sell your data to third parties
  • ❌ We do NOT share data with advertisers
  • ❌ We do NOT collect precise location data
  • ❌ We do NOT access your contacts or photos
  • ❌ We do NOT see your payment information (handled by Google Play)

👥 Third-Party Services

  • Google Play Store: For app distribution and in-app purchases
  • Supabase: For secure database storage
  • GitHub Pages: For hosting our website
