🗑️ 8Eggs Account & Data Deletion Request

⚠️ IMPORTANT: Account deletion is PERMANENT and CANNOT BE UNDONE. All your game progress, scores, and data will be lost forever.

📋 What Data We Store About You

  • Display Name: Your chosen player name for leaderboards
  • User ID: Anonymous identifier for your account
  • Game Progress: All levels, scores, and achievements
  • Leaderboard Entries: Your rankings and scores
  • Friend Referrals: Your referral history and codes
  • Purchase Records: In-app purchase history (anonymized)

🗑️ What Gets Deleted

When you request deletion, we will permanently remove:

  • ✅ Your display name from our databases
  • ✅ All your game progress and scores
  • ✅ All leaderboard entries associated with you
  • ✅ Your friend referral data
  • ✅ Your complete user account record
  • ✅ Any stored game preferences or settings

⏱️ Data Retention Timeline

Processing Time: Deletion requests are processed within 7 days.

Complete Removal: Data is permanently erased from our databases within 30 days of your request.

Backup Retention: Encrypted backups may retain data for up to 90 days before automatic deletion.

Step 2: Include Required Information

Your email MUST include:

  • Confirmation: That you understand this is permanent
  • Optional: Your User ID (found in game settings)

Step 3: Wait for Confirmation

We will email you a confirmation within 7 business days once your data has been scheduled for deletion.

❓ Partial Data Deletion

If you only want specific data removed (not your entire account), mention this in your email. We can:

  • Remove only your display name from leaderboards
  • Delete specific game progress data
  • Remove you from friend referral systems

Note: Partial deletion may affect game functionality.
